# How hit Chinese micro-dramas (短剧) are written — research for Photo Wahala

Research brief, 6 October 2026. It is the reason behind the rules in
`COPY-FORMULA.md`. Sources are cited inline. Unverifiable statistics are flagged
at the end; several Chinese tutorials (Zhihu, Medium) and the Guardian Nigeria
returned 403 and could not be read.

## 1. The opening — the "golden 3 seconds" (黄金三秒)
- Attention is judged at 3s, 10s, 1 min and 3 min; the first 3 seconds decide the
  scroll ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/899468839_693376)). Showrunners call it the
  爆点, "the detonation" ([Story Farm](https://thestoryfarm.substack.com/p/the-shortest-story-still-has-a-soul)).
- Begin after the blow: "The slap has already landed… The bride has already
  bolted." A cold open shows a face reacting, a shocking line, or a reveal in
  motion ([invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/)).
- Strong: a slap landing; a will naming the despised one as heir; a door kicked
  open on a secret; a line contradicting last episode's ending. Weak: skylines,
  inner monologue, recap narration, voice-over introductions
  ([Maosika](https://www.maosika.com/blog/vertical-short-drama-script-handbook-2),
  [InkTip](https://www.inktip.com/articles/writing-for-vertical-video-scripts)).
- Core conflict established within ~30 seconds of Episode 1
  ([Hailuoshe](https://www.hailuoshe.com/blog/short-drama-script-writing)).
- Example: ReelShort's *The Double Life of My Billionaire Husband* opens with
  Natalie begging her father for $50,000 for her mother's bills, blocked by the
  stepmother and stepsister, pushed into marrying the "ex-convict" who is secretly
  a billionaire — humiliation, forced choice and hidden identity in Episode 1
  ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/tv-movies/the-double-life-of-my-billionaire-husband-ending-explained-2454997/)).

## 2. 爽点 — satisfaction points, and the face-slap cycle
- 爽点 = a moment of emotional payoff; the core is 解气, releasing pent-up
  anger, usually through 打脸, the face-slap that humiliates the villain.
- Three beats: verbal aggression → the reversal revealed → the villain crushed
  ([invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/)).
- Series curve 先抑后扬, hold down then lift: frustration → small relief →
  bigger frustration → big payoff ([Hailuoshe](https://www.hailuoshe.com/blog/short-drama-script-writing)).
- At least one small payoff per episode; a big one every 3–5 episodes
  ([Maosika](https://www.maosika.com/blog/vertical-short-drama-script-handbook-2));
  "satisfaction density" ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1075899825_123005834)).
- Payoff kinds: face-slap, comeback, sweetness, heartbreak, suspense, rousing,
  comedy, tenderness ([0xsline/short-drama](https://github.com/0xsline/short-drama),
  [The World of Chinese](https://www.theworldofchinese.com/2025/09/welcome-to-the-micro-drama-nation-china/)).
- The villain gets temporary wins first, to open an "anxiety gap" before the fall
  ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6191708.html)).

## 3. Reversals (反转) and hidden identities
- The double-identity engine: a low visible role (guard, rider, live-in
  son-in-law 赘婿) hiding a powerful one
  ([Digitaling, 120 tropes](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1281671.html)).
- Pressure through relationships (bride price, the spouse who sides with family).
- Why it works: 爽 is the first principle; "playing the pig to eat the tiger"
  (扮猪吃老虎) compensates the bullied; one readable label per character
  ([The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_25554154)).
- Twists must be "fast, harsh and reasonable", with small clues planted first so
  the viewer thinks "so that's how it was"
  ([lmtw](https://lmtw.com/mzw/content/detail/id/241607/keyword_id/-1)). A reveal
  with no consequences, or a payoff with no setup, is a named failure
  ([Maosika](https://www.maosika.com/blog/vertical-short-drama-script-handbook-2)).

## 4. Cliffhangers (钩子 / 卡点) at the end of every episode
- Kinds: rhetorical question, interrupted event, reversed information
  ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1075899825_123005834)); suspense, emotion,
  information, crisis, reversal ([0xsline](https://github.com/0xsline/short-drama)).
- Cut while the emotion is unresolved or the plot has just turned
  ([lmtw](https://lmtw.com/mzw/content/detail/id/241607/keyword_id/-1)).
- Producers want a Revelation, a Reversal, a Deadline or an Intrusion
  ([InkTip](https://www.inktip.com/articles/writing-for-vertical-video-scripts)).
- Strongest: power reversal and information (a document or photo half-shown).
  Weakest: fake tension — gasping at something never shown. "To be continued"
  with no new turn "is not a cliffhanger — it's a missing scene"
  ([Maosika](https://www.maosika.com/blog/vertical-short-drama-script-handbook-2)).
- Write each episode's ending first, then build toward it
  ([invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/)).

## 5. Pacing
- Episodes of 60–120 s; ReelShort asks for 1–2 min, 60–80 per project
  ([job post](https://startup.jobs/screenwriter-reelshort-contractor-crazy-maple-studio-6258704)).
- One episode: 0–5 s hook / 5–40 s escalation / 40–75 s small payoff / 75–90 s
  cliffhanger ([Hailuoshe](https://www.hailuoshe.com/blog/short-drama-script-writing));
  exactly one reversal in the middle ([invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/)).
- One engine: a simple goal, a recurring obstacle, time pressure, an open
  question ([InkTip](https://www.inktip.com/articles/writing-for-vertical-video-scripts)).
  "Enter scenes late and leave early" ([Jon Stahl](https://hownot.substack.com/p/micro-series-a-manifesto)).

## 6. Characters and dialogue
- Small casts — often two protagonists
  ([Script Mag](https://scriptmag.com/true-indie-writing-for-verticals-interview-with-screenwriter-lynn-grant-beck));
  more than 2–3 central characters "create tracking problems".
- One label each: kind heroine, vicious sister, biased stepmother
  ([The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_25554154)).
- Lines of at most 15–20 Chinese characters, one thought each, no lecturing, no
  narrator; "read aloud on mobile while scrolling — if you skip lines, cut them"
  ([Hailuoshe](https://www.hailuoshe.com/blog/short-drama-script-writing),
  [Maosika](https://www.maosika.com/blog/vertical-short-drama-script-handbook-2)).
  A quotable line (金句) per episode ([0xsline](https://github.com/0xsline/short-drama)).
- "The vertical frame loves faces, hands, and intimacy"
  ([InkTip](https://www.inktip.com/articles/writing-for-vertical-video-scripts)).

## 7. Western adaptations
- ReelShort moved from translations to local originals with US casts and writers
  ([Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2023/what-is-reelshort/)); war gods
  became CEOs and alphas; the 赘婿 template survives.
- Audience is mostly women, especially urban women of 30–60
  ([Story Farm](https://thestoryfarm.substack.com/p/the-shortest-story-still-has-a-soul)).
- China is pulling back from pure 爽: the regulator is targeting outrageous plots
  and clickbait ([The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_29850576));
  "anti-trope within trope" heroines who refuse to suffer quietly are rising
  ([World of Chinese](https://www.theworldofchinese.com/2025/09/welcome-to-the-micro-drama-nation-china/));
  predictability is starting to kill engagement
  ([Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1281671.html)).

## 8. Nigeria and static carousels
- Nigeria is a top-5 market for these apps; werewolf and European fantasy tropes
  "don't resonate locally"; Nollywood's dialogue-driven instincts fit the format
  ([Communiqué](https://www.readcommunique.com/p/nollywood-billion-dollar-microdrama-opportunity)).
- Local equivalents: the looked-down-on "small boy" or quiet relative for the
  赘婿; the big madam or Lagos aunty for the CEO; the mother-in-law; owambe,
  wedding and naming-ceremony set pieces for the banquet; wahala for the 虐点.
- Comics map to carousels: the hook in panel 1, the punchline or cliffhanger in
  the last ([Artfolio](https://www.artfolio.com/article/cartoonist-storytelling-sprints-pacing-secrets-to-keep-readers-hooked)).

## 9. Common mistakes
A slow first episode; a pleasant story with no real conflict; too many
characters; narrated recaps; "reset" episodes that cancel the last cliffhanger;
fake tension; reveals with no consequence; hollow archetypes; predictable tropes
with no twist on the twist; payoffs that never come.
