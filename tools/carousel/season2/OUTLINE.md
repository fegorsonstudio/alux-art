# Photo Wahala — Season 2 · the writers' room

All of the account's carousels are written in the Photo Wahala microdrama
style (the owner's decision, 2026-10-06). Season 2 replaces the 88 older
drafts: 22 stories, one a week, four episodes each, posting Monday, Wednesday,
Friday and Sunday at **7pm Lagos**, in the dated slots those drafts held
(`p01`–`p88`). Season 1 — `plan-microdrama.mjs`, 11 stories — posts at 1pm.

Story `n` is week `n`; episode `e` is post `p((n−1)×4+e)`. Episode 1 is the
Monday, 2 Wednesday, 3 Friday, 4 Sunday.

**Four episodes land on their day and must say so:** Story 12 Ep 3 is
**Christmas Day** (Fri 25 Dec), Story 13 Ep 3 is **New Year's Day** (Fri 1 Jan),
Story 19 Ep 4 is **Valentine's Day** (Sun 14 Feb), Story 22 Ep 4 is
**Mothering Sunday** (Sun 7 Mar) and the season finale.

Product mix follows the old drafts: relight 9 stories, studio (the Gear
Equalizer) 6, the full shoot 3, the Asset Extractor 2, the GIF sticker 2.

Each Ep 4 ends on the NEXT story's hook (the two lines listed under it), as
Season 1 does: `["Next Monday on Photo Wahala", hook line 1, hook line 2]`.
