## Order to apply

1. **Microsoft for Startups (self-serve)** — $1,000 of Azure credit at once, up
   to $5,000 after a business check (company registration). Credits run 90/180
   days. GPU machines need a quota request (say: "Alux Art is in Microsoft for
   Startups; we need 1 GPU (NVads A10 v5 or NCasT4_v3) for Unreal Engine rendering").
2. **AWS Activate — Founders** — $1,000 (+ support credit) for a self-funded
   company with a live website and an AWS account. Needs a card on the AWS account.
3. **Google for Startups Cloud — Start** — from $2,000 with a working product.
   (The ordinary $300 Google trial does NOT allow GPU machines.)
4. **NVIDIA Inception** — free membership, discounts and partner offers (no cash).
5. **Epic MegaGrants** — $5,000–$75,000 grant for Unreal projects; the 2026 windows
   closed on 4 September — prepare for the next one.

Use one at a time: the studio machine is switched on only while pictures are made.
