SCENE CONTEXT




The accountant carries the stack of magazines toward the table, trips on the paper runway and goes down. Her glasses fly off, the hair tie snaps, and her hair falls completely loose. The head of the house gives a tired "here we go again" look. Then she lifts her face into the evening light, and she is beautiful. He looks at her and thinks, the pen stops, and he closes the portfolio. She brushes the hair from her mouth and reaches for a page.




ACTIVE REFERENCES




@Image 1 — LOCATION LOCK: the exact room from this image, unchanged.




@Image 2 RAPHAEL: French man, mid-50s, lean, head of the house; seated in the red chair at the north corner of the white Parsons table, chair turned out toward the room, legs crossed knee over knee, an open portfolio on the table at his left elbow, a slim silver pen turning slowly in his right fingers; weary, gentle, unhurried; stays seated throughout. Face, hair and suit 100% match the reference.




 @Image 3 REBECCA: woman, about 30, his secretary, standing at the table at his shoulder, clipboard against her chest, pen in hand; never leaves the table. 100% matches the reference.




 @Image 4 LUCY: woman, 23, junior accountant, glasses on at the start, wearing the same open pale-blue cardigan over a light tank, checked skirt, shoes and necklace as @Image 4; face, glasses and outfit 100% match the reference. Her hair stays in the low bun until 3.6s; the bun comes apart from 3.6s to 4.8s; from 4.8s to the end her hair is completely loose around her face and over her shoulders. Her glasses begin sliding off at 3.3s, leave her face by 4.0s and have landed on the paper by 5.5s.




The room holds only these three people; the wall by the glass partition is empty and the door is open with nobody in it.




LOCATION MAP (compass as in @Image 1)




West wall: tall arched windows with sconces, racks, dummy and mirror. North wall: the glass partition with moodboards and the open double door, cardboard boxes just east of the door. East side: white seamless backdrop and camera on tripod. South end: the white Parsons table; RAPHAEL in the red chair at its north corner facing north, REBECCA at his shoulder. The cream paper runway runs north from the table toward the door. LUCY walks south along the west side of the runway and goes down at its west edge, 5 m north of RAPHAEL, in the brightest pool of window light, falling toward the windows. All cameras stay east of the north–south axis except shot 2. East-side rule: RAPHAEL, REBECCA and the table screen-left, LUCY screen-right with the windows behind her; RAPHAEL's gaze to LUCY is screen-right.




FIRST FRAME




WIDE from the east: RAPHAEL seated at the table screen-left, x 20%, REBECCA at his shoulder; LUCY screen-right, x 75%, two steps south of the boxes, the magazine stack chin-high against her folder, glasses on, hair in the loose bun; windows glowing behind her.




FORMAT MODE




Six segments with HARD CUTs. Shot 1 and shot 3 at normal speed; shots 2, 4, 5, 6 at 30% slow motion. No dialogue.




SHOT LIST — OPTICS AND CAMERA (all locked-off tripod, horizon level)




1. 0.0–2.5s — WIDE. 47° standard normal, camera 5 m east of the runway at 1.4 m beside the white backdrop, looking west; the table with RAPHAEL and REBECCA screen-left, LUCY screen-right, windows blooming.




2. 2.5–5.5s — THE FALL FROM THE WINDOW SIDE, 30% speed. 29°, the only west-side camera: 2 m west of her landing point, lens 0.6 m above the floor, looking east; LUCY alone in frame, framed from head to hips, falling toward the lens; her face and the flying glasses sharp, the racks and the white backdrop a warm blur behind her.




3. 5.5–7.0s — CLOSE-UP RAPHAEL, normal speed. 29°, camera 2.5 m east of him at seated eye level; his face and the pen sharp, REBECCA a soft shape at the frame edge screen-right.




4. 7.0–10.0s — CLOSE-UP LUCY LIFTING HER FACE, 30% speed. 29° short telephoto, camera 2.5 m east of her, lens at her eye level; a tight head close-up framed from the top of her head down to her collarbones, the open cardigan and light tank neckline at the bottom of the frame; the windows a warm glowing blur behind her.




5. 10.0–12.5s — CLOSE-UP RAPHAEL, 30% speed. Same camera as shot 3; his face, the pen and the open portfolio sharp.




6. 12.5–15.0s — CLOSE-UP LUCY ON THE FLOOR, 30% speed. 29°, camera 2.5 m east of her at 0.8 m; framed from her head down to her hands on the floor, cardigan worn open; her face, hands and the nearest pages sharp.




ACTION TIMING




0.0s to 2.5s — WIDE. LUCY walks south along the west side of the runway with quick uneven steps, the stack chin-high, glancing over it at the table (0.0s to 1.2s). The top magazine slides sideways off the stack (1.2s to 1.5s); she tries to save it with her elbow and the whole stack tips (1.5s to 2.0s); her shoe catches the taped edge of the paper runway, the paper tears at the tape, and she pitches forward toward the windows, arms opening (2.0s to 2.5s). REBECCA watches from the table, frozen.




2.5s HARD CUT — SLOW MOTION




2.5s to 5.5s — THE FALL, 30% speed. LUCY comes down toward the lens: the stack leaves her arms and the magazines fan open in the air, the folder yawns and white pages lift around her (2.5s to 3.3s); the glasses slide off her nose, hang for a beat and tumble past the lens out of frame (3.3s to 4.0s); the hair tie snaps and flies off, the bun comes completely apart, and all her hair falls free in one slow heavy wave, forward around her face and over her shoulders (3.6s to 4.8s); her palms reach the floor, then her knees (4.8s to 5.5s). She ends on her hands and knees, head down, her loose hair hanging like a curtain around her face. Pages settle around her like leaves.




5.5s HARD CUT — NORMAL SPEED




5.5s to 7.0s — CLOSE-UP RAPHAEL. The tired "here we go again" face: he looks screen-right at the noise, his eyelids drop halfway, a slow exhale through the nose, the corners of his mouth pull down, and his head tilts a few degrees in weary resignation; the pen keeps turning in his fingers, lazily. REBECCA at the frame edge presses the clipboard to her chest.




7.0s HARD CUT — SLOW MOTION




7.0s to 10.0s — CLOSE-UP LUCY, 30% speed. Slowly and gracefully she lifts her head: the loose hair slides back off her face and settles over her shoulders, strands backlit with a golden rim (7.0s to 8.5s); her face rises into the low evening sun — flushed cheeks, parted lips, wide clear eyes without glasses, window catchlights in them; soft, unguarded, luminous, completely beautiful, completely unaware of it (8.5s to 10.0s). She looks down at the scattered pages, not at anyone.




10.0s HARD CUT




10.0s to 12.5s — CLOSE-UP RAPHAEL, 30% speed. The weariness drains out of his face. The pen slows and stops between his fingers (10.0s to 10.6s). He looks at LUCY screen-right, eyes narrowing slightly, thinking, as if seeing something for the first time (10.6s to 11.4s); his eyes drop to the open portfolio (11.4s to 11.8s), rise back to her (11.8s to 12.1s); his left hand closes the portfolio slowly, the cover settling under its own weight (12.1s to 12.5s). REBECCA at the frame edge has gone completely still.




12.5s HARD CUT




12.5s to 15.0s — CLOSE-UP LUCY, 30% speed. A shaft of low sun lies across her face. Loose strands cling to her lip; she brushes them away with the back of her hand (12.5s to 13.5s). A page slides across the floor toward her; she reaches for it and the long cardigan sleeve slips down over her fingers; she tugs it back and gathers the page, fully occupied with the mess (13.5s to 15.0s). Her glasses lie on the runway paper beside her, out of reach. Hold.




ACTING TASK — RAPHAEL: DIRECTION: get through another empty day. MOTIVE: everything in this room is rehearsed and he has stopped seeing it. GOAL: find something real to look at. OBSTACLE: he expects nothing from this room. TACTIC: the fall is just one more small disaster — a tired look, an exhale, the pen still turning; when she lifts her face his tiredness is gone before he notices it; he studies her like a question, and closing the portfolio is a decision made in silence.




ACTING TASK — REBECCA: DIRECTION: the same. MOTIVE: his mood is her job. GOAL: stay invisible. TACTIC: she freezes at his shoulder, because she does not yet know whose fault this is.




ACTING TASK — LUCY: DIRECTION: nobody makes a scene. MOTIVE: she is new and only came for a signature. GOAL: fix it and get out. OBSTACLE: her arms, the magazine, the paper edge, her own sleeves. TACTIC: in the fall she protects nothing; lifting her face is simply checking whether the world is still there; then she goes straight to cleaning up; she has no idea she is being looked at.




(Safety: gazes engaged, natural blink cadence, mouths closed, nobody speaks.)




PHYSICS




The stack has weight: her arms sink under it and she rebalances with her chin. The top magazine slides on the glossy cover below it; the elbow save tips the stack; the shoe catches the taped edge and the paper tears. The magazines fan out and slap the floor; the folder opens and light pages plane and scatter. The glasses leave her face in one piece, tumble and skid on the paper. The elastic hair tie snaps free and the bun uncoils under its own weight; the hair falls fully loose in one heavy wave and swings as her head lifts. Palms then knees take the floor with weight. Hair strands stick to her lip and lift with the back of her hand; the sleeve slides down over her knuckles. In slow motion everything keeps its weight: pages settling, hair swaying, the pen stopping, the portfolio cover falling closed.

In shots 4 and 6 a single shaft of low sun across her face, loose strands backlit with a golden rim, window catchlights in her eyes. Exposed for skin, windows allowed to bloom.

From 2.5s the sound stretches with the picture: slow flutter and slap of magazines and pages, the glasses skidding, knees on oak. Shot 3 at normal speed: RAPHAEL's soft exhale through the nose, the pen ticking. Shots 4–6: low stretched room tone, the pen stopping, the soft thud of the portfolio cover, a page sliding on oak. Nobody speaks. Score is added in the edit.
