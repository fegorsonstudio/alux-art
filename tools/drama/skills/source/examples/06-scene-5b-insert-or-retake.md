# Their example 6 — from the Higgsfield blog (SOUL Angel)

## The lesson around it



PACE — SPEECH RIDES ON TOP OF ACTION




Nothing in this clip waits for anything else. Every line begins while the movement that goes with it is still happening, not after it finishes: he speaks as he arrives, as his hands close, as he turns away, as he walks. Nobody completes a gesture, pauses, and then talks. There is no dead air anywhere — no gap between one person finishing and the next starting, and no held silence between a movement and a line. The whole clip is brisk and overlapped.

## The clip prompt, verbatim

```
SCENE CONTEXT




The head stylist walks into the showroom still on a phone call, shown from behind first — the phone at his ear, the walk, the hand — before we ever see his face. As he reaches them the room rearranges around him: the girl turns, the boss gets up off his table, the assistant gathers her papers and quietly leaves. The stylist announces himself with both arms thrown open, on the move. Nobody welcomes him: the head of the house crosses to the girl, places both hands over hers while she supports the magazines against her chest and talks about her to the other man, then lets go and walks out mid-sentence. Left alone with her, the stylist reads her head to foot in one quick look, lifts a strand of her hair, holds out his hands for the stack she has been clutching all morning, takes it off her, tells her his name, and walks her out.




LUCY'S LINE THROUGH THE CLIP




Everything that happens to her happens in her face, her breathing and her hands. She never protests and never says anything she is not scripted to say.




 - While LUCA arrives and announces himself — she has no idea who this man is and no idea whether the arms-open thing is a joke she is supposed to laugh at. She does not smile. She watches him the way you watch a dog you have not been introduced to.




 - When RAPHAEL places his hands over hers — for about a second something real happens: her shoulders drop, her eyes soften, she is genuinely touched, because this is the first time all morning anyone has treated her like a person. Then he starts speaking about her in the third person to the other man, and the warmth goes out of her face while his hands are still resting over hers. She does not pull away. She just stops being warm.




 - On "Find her something beautiful to wear" — her eyes drop to her own cardigan and skirt and come straight back up.




 - While RAPHAEL walks out — she watches him the whole way. Her lips part very slightly as if to say something to stop him, and she does not.




 - Under LUCA's quick look up and down — her arms tighten around the magazines and her weight shifts a fraction back onto her heels.




 - When he lifts the strand of her hair — she goes completely still and stops breathing for a beat, eyes fixed forward, not daring to move her head.




 - When he holds out his hands for the stack — the hardest thing in the clip for her. The magazines are her errand, her job, her reason to be allowed in this room; handing them over is handing over the third floor. She hesitates, then gives it up, and something visibly goes out of her as the weight leaves her arms.




 - With her arms empty — she does not know what to do with her hands. They hang, half rise, and settle.




 - On "Luca." / "Lucy." — the first kind thing in two minutes. Something eases a fraction; the first moment she has been spoken to rather than about.




 - On the last step after him — a decision, and it is visible: one breath, then she moves.




EVERYTHING IS BLANK AND UNBRANDED




Every surface that could carry a mark is smooth and empty. The magazines in LUCY's arms and the magazines on the table all carry a photographed face on smooth empty paper and nothing else — no title, masthead, word, letter, numeral, logo, barcode or cover line anywhere on any cover, spine, edge or page. REBECCA's papers are plain unprinted sheets. The pinned photographs on the mood board are pictures on blank paper. Phone casings, rings, garments, boots and furniture are plain and unmarked. No real product or company appears anywhere in any frame.




ONE TABLE ONLY




There is exactly one table in this room: the long pale work table from @Image 1, in the near foreground screen-LEFT, running away from camera, with magazines, a desk lamp, peonies, a corded telephone and a chair. No second desk, side table or workstation exists anywhere, and nothing stands against the walls.




LUCA'S ENTRANCE — FIVE SHOTS, IN THIS ORDER, BACK BEFORE FRONT




1. FROM BEHIND, WALKING AND TALKING. Three-quarter rear at head height: the back of his head, his shoulder, his raised arm, the slim silver flip phone at his right ear, the near edge of his cheek and jaw visible past his shoulder as he speaks. He is walking the whole time. REAL TIME.




2. FROM BEHIND, LOWER AND WIDER. The back of his hips and the line of his trousers fill the centre of the frame as he keeps walking away down the room, the weight rolling from one hip to the other. He walks like a man who is being photographed. SLOW MOTION, silent.




3. HIS HAND at his hip, the rings catching the light. SLOW MOTION, silent.




4. HIS EYES, from the front — a horizontal strip across both eyes, the sharp black winged liner fully legible. SLOW MOTION, silent.




5. HIS FACE, from the front, whole for the first time, pocketing the phone. REAL TIME.




ACTIVE REFERENCES




@Image 1 — location: the room's geometry, its furniture and where it stands, its doorway at the far end, its materials, its window-light direction.




@Image 2 LUCY: woman, 23, junior accountant. Loose messy mid-length hair to her shoulder blades, hair down, no glasses on her face and none anywhere on her body. Pale blue cardigan over a light tank, dark plaid mini skirt, bare legs, black pointed kitten heels, one thin silver chain. Until 20.1s she securely supports a stack of glossy magazines against her chest with her arms and hands. RAPHAEL can briefly place his hands over hers without interrupting her support of the stack. She deliberately transfers the stack to LUCA from 20.1s to 21.7s; her hands are empty after 21.7s. Nothing hangs from either elbow or shoulder; both forearms are free of straps. Face, hair and outfit 100% match the reference.




@Image 3 RAPHAEL: man, mid-50s, lean, head of the house. Warm, dry, contained; his mouth is mostly still and he does not smile in this clip. Face, hair and suit 100% match the reference.




@Image 4 REBECCA: woman, about 30, the assistant. Starts seated at the long table with her back to camera, head down over a stack of plain unprinted sheets. Soft and out of focus, never the subject of a shot, never speaks. She gathers the papers, stands and walks out, always seen from behind. Hair and clothing 100% match the reference.




@Image 5 LUCA: man, early 30s, head stylist, beautiful and entirely aware of it. Sharp black winged eyeliner, one clean flick at each outer corner. Rings on the fingers of both hands. Black boots. He arrives carrying nothing but a slim silver flip phone at his right ear, which he snaps shut and pockets. Face, hair and outfit 100% match the reference.




These four are the only people in this clip. Nobody else is ever visible anywhere in it.




HOW TO USE THE CHARACTER REFERENCES




Take only the face, the hair and the clothing from each reference. Do not take its lighting, background, wall colour or camera angle. All four are lit by this scene's own window light, in the room from @Image 1.




LOCATION MAP




The showroom from @Image 1: pale walls, clean pale stone floor, a long cream runner down the middle, tall arched windows with trees beyond them on the far side, chrome racks of garments, a dress form, a tall leaning mirror, a mood board, a doorway at the far end.




Camera on the east side throughout.




AT THE START: RAPHAEL sits on the edge of the long table in the near foreground screen-LEFT with his arms folded; LUCY stands alone on the open floor in the middle of the room, screen-RIGHT of him and deeper in, facing screen-left toward him; REBECCA is a blurred back seated at that table. LUCA comes from the doorway at the far end, behind LUCY.




THE REGROUPING at 7.6s: LUCY turns her body away from RAPHAEL to face the space between the two men; RAPHAEL pushes up off the table edge and comes forward onto the floor to stand; LUCA arrives and stops. From 9.8s the arrangement is fixed: RAPHAEL screen-LEFT, LUCY CENTRE, LUCA screen-RIGHT, all three standing.




REBECCA leaves during the regrouping, quietly and in the background.




From 17.5s RAPHAEL is gone and only LUCY and LUCA remain, facing each other about a metre apart, LUCY screen-left of frame centre and LUCA screen-right. LUCY does not take a step until the very last second of the clip.




FORMAT MODE




Aspect ratio 4:3, 1440 x 1080, landscape. Ten segments, 26.2 seconds. Real-time motion everywhere except segments 2, 3 and 4, which are in slow motion and silent. The shot size changes at every cut. All cuts are HARD CUTS.




OPTICS AND CAMERA




Seg 1: 29°, camera behind LUCA at 2 m, head height 1.6 m, three-quarter rear. The camera MOVES with him at his walking pace, steady as a Steadicam, holding the same distance.




Seg 2: 29° from behind at 2.5 m, camera at 1.0 m height. Locked. Slow motion.




Seg 3: 18° tight on his hand at his hip, camera 2 m, the rings sharp. Locked. Slow motion.




Seg 4: 18° extreme close-up from the front, camera 3 m — a horizontal strip across both eyes. Locked. Slow motion.




Seg 5: 29° from the front, camera 3 m at 1.4 m — his whole face, head and shoulders. Locked.




Seg 6: 47° wide, camera 5 m at 1.4 m, wide enough to hold his fully opened arms inside the frame and to keep the table and REBECCA's exit readable. Locked.




Seg 7: 47°, camera 4 m at 1.4 m, three-quarter figures of the standing three, close enough that the joined hands are clearly readable and LUCY's face is fully legible. Locked.




Seg 8: 47° wide, camera 5.5 m at 1.4 m, holding the depth of the room so RAPHAEL can recede toward the far doorway, with LUCY in frame watching him go. Locked.




Seg 9: 29° OVER-THE-SHOULDER on LUCA, camera 2 m, just past LUCY's right shoulder — her shoulder and the edge of her hair a soft dark shape filling the near screen-left quarter, LUCA's face razor-sharp beyond it. Locked.




Seg 10: 29° tight two-shot in profile, camera 2.5 m at 1.5 m, LUCY screen-left and LUCA screen-right, both faces sharp and both mouths clearly visible. Locked.




No zoom anywhere. The camera moves only in segment 1.




ACTION TIMING




0.0–2.6 — seg 1, FROM BEHIND. LUCA walks in with the phone at his right ear, finishing a call that started long before this scene, warm and offhand: "Yes, handsome. Eight o'clock. I remember." (0.2–2.4). He walks throughout and never breaks stride.




2.6 HARD CUT




2.6–4.0 — seg 2, FROM BEHIND, his hips and the line of his trousers as he keeps walking away down the room. Slow motion, silent.




4.0 HARD CUT




4.0–4.9 — seg 3, HIS HAND at his hip, the rings catching the light. Slow motion, silent.




4.9 HARD CUT




4.9–5.9 — seg 4, HIS EYES from the front, the winged liner sharp. They lift, one blink, and travel across the room. Slow motion, silent.




5.9 HARD CUT




5.9–7.6 — seg 5, HIS FACE from the front, whole for the first time. Still walking, he brings the phone down, snaps it shut one-handed with a crisp click (6.5–6.9) and slides it into his trouser pocket (6.9–7.3). Both hands free, eyes on the two of them.




7.6 HARD CUT




7.6–10.8 — seg 6, wide, one continuous overlapping action. LUCA is still walking the last few metres (7.6–8.6). While he does, LUCY turns her body away from RAPHAEL to face the space between the two men (7.7–8.4), RAPHAEL pushes up off the table edge and comes forward onto the floor (7.9–9.0), and behind them, unwatched, REBECCA squares her papers, stands and walks out of the room, seen only from behind (7.8–10.3).




LUCA does not stop and then announce himself — the arms come open on the move: both swing wide from the shoulders, palms forward and empty, chest lifting, chin up, as he takes his last step (8.5–8.9). He says it with the arms already out, languidly, unhurried, in the third person, entirely pleased with himself and entirely aware that it is funny: "Luca is here." (9.0–10.1). The arms drop after (10.1–10.8). He is looking at LUCY while he says it, not at RAPHAEL. She does not smile.




10.8 HARD CUT




10.8–14.5 — seg 7. Nobody welcomes him. RAPHAEL steps across to LUCY and his palms close warmly over the backs of her hands where they hold the stack against her chest (10.8–11.5). For a second she is genuinely touched — shoulders down, eyes soft. He does not wait for the gesture to finish before speaking: the line starts while his hands are still closing, and he speaks past her, to LUCA: "Our new face. My muse." (11.2–12.3). The warmth drains out of her face while he is still holding on. He lets go (12.3–12.6), and straight on, already beginning to turn: "Find her something beautiful to wear." (12.6–13.9). On those words her eyes drop to her own cardigan and skirt and come back up (13.4–13.8). He turns his back on both of them (13.9–14.5).




14.5 HARD CUT




14.5–17.5 — seg 8, wide. RAPHAEL is already walking away down the length of the room toward the far doorway, unhurried, never looking back, and the line comes while he walks: without stopping and without turning his body he turns his head back over his shoulder so his face is clearly visible to camera and adds it as an afterthought, lifting the end into a question: "And do something with her hair, yes?" (14.9–16.1). He does not wait for an answer, reaches the doorway and goes out (16.1–17.5). LUCY watches him the whole way; her lips part very slightly as if to stop him, and she does not.




17.5 HARD CUT




17.5–21.7 — seg 9, over LUCY's shoulder onto LUCA. They are alone. Nobody speaks in this segment, and the four beats run straight into each other with no pauses between them.




 - 17.5–18.4 — he reads her in one quick pass: his eyes drop from her hair down the cardigan, the skirt, the bare legs, the shoes, and come straight back to her face. Fast and unembarrassed, the way you check a garment on a rail. Everything he thinks about the outfit stays in his eyes. Her arms tighten around the magazines.




 - 18.4–19.6 — he reaches up and lifts one strand of her hair away from her shoulder between two fingers, turns it once in the window light, and lets it fall back. She goes completely still and stops breathing for a beat.




 - 19.6–20.1 — his hands come out toward her, palms up and open, asking without a word for the stack.




 - 20.1–21.7 — she looks at his hands, then at the stack, hesitates, and gives it up.




21.7 HARD CUT




21.7–26.2 — seg 10, tight two-shot in profile, the final shot of the clip. LUCA has the stack tucked under one arm. LUCY's arms are empty for the first time all morning and she does not know where to put them; they hang, half rise, settle, and something has visibly gone out of her with the weight (21.7–22.5). LUCA, offering it rather than announcing it: "Luca." (22.6–23.0). She works out that this is her turn — something eases a fraction — and answers, small: "Lucy." (23.3–23.8). He tips his head toward the far end of the room, warm and matter-of-fact, and is already turning as he says it: "Come with me, Lucy." (24.1–25.2). He walks. She takes one breath, and takes one first step after him, and the clip ends there (25.2–26.2).




DIALOGUE PLACEMENT RULE




Every spoken line belongs entirely to one segment and is delivered on camera by the speaker. No line starts in one segment and finishes in another, and no line is heard while the speaker is out of frame. In segment 1 LUCA is seen from behind in three-quarter rear with the phone at his ear; the near edge of his cheek and jaw is visible past his shoulder and his line is delivered there, with his jaw moving as he speaks. In segment 8 RAPHAEL stays inside the frame and turns his head back toward camera so his mouth is visible while he says his line. In segment 10 both mouths are clearly visible in profile. Each sentence starts and finishes inside its own shot at one steady close recording level, with no fade and no trailing off at any cut. Segments 2, 3, 4, 5 and 9 contain no dialogue whatsoever. REBECCA never speaks.




ACTING




LUCA — the most beautiful thing that has walked into this room today, and he has never once had to wonder about it. He finishes his own life on the phone before giving the room a second of attention. "Luca is here" is a joke he is making about himself and he knows exactly how funny it is: wide, slow and delighted, not mad. Alone with her he stops performing and starts working — the fast look, the strand of hair and the open hands are all one act, a man taking inventory. He is not cruel about the outfit and does not need to be; he has already replaced it in his head. Taking the stack off her is the first kind thing anyone has done for her in this scene and he does not make a speech about it. He is the only person here who tells her his name.




RAPHAEL — he decided long ago and is no longer selling anything. He does not greet Luca and does not answer him. Taking her hands is meant kindly and it is: he is genuinely fond of her and this is how he says congratulations. And while he holds her hands he talks about her to another man in the third person, and does not notice that the two things do not go together. He is already leaving while he finishes his last order. No smile.




REBECCA — she reads the room, decides she is not needed, and takes herself out of it without being asked.




(Safety: gazes engaged, natural blink cadence, listeners' mouths closed.)




PHYSICS




Until 20.1s the magazine stack stays securely supported against LUCY's chest, including while RAPHAEL places his hands over hers. There is no accidental slipping or dropping. At 20.1–21.7 she deliberately transfers the stack with real weight out of her arms into LUCA's hands, glossy covers sliding against each other, and he settles it under one arm. Her arms rise a couple of centimetres when the weight leaves them. When she turns her hair swings across her shoulder and settles. RAPHAEL's hands close over hers with real contact and part cleanly. The strand of hair LUCA lifts has real weight: it separates, hangs from his fingers, falls back against her shoulder and settles. LUCA's boots land with real weight on stone; when his arms swing open his jacket opens with them. The flip phone snaps shut with a real spring. REBECCA's chair slides back on stone as she stands. In the slow-motion shots the movement is smooth and weighted, never floaty.




The air in the room is completely still. Nothing is blowing, lifting, floating, flying or caught in a breeze.




LIGHTING




Afternoon sun through the tall arched windows is the only source. Faces are lit by soft bounce off the pale walls and floor with a warm rim along hair and shoulders. LUCA's rear shots are backlit so his shoulders carry a bright rim; his close-ups are lit by the same warm daylight, eyes clean and open with the liner crisply readable, rings catching a hard little highlight. The far doorway is in softer shade, so RAPHAEL darkens as he walks out. LUCY stands inside a shaft of sun, rim-lit along her hair and shoulders, her face filled by bounce off the pale floor so every change in it is readable; when LUCA lifts the strand of hair it glows against the window light. Exposure is honest: the windows are not blown out, the trees outside stay visible and keep their colour. No studio lamp, softbox, reflector, stand, flag, cable or crew is visible anywhere in the frame, in any corner, at any moment.




AUDIO




Voices close and clean over quiet room tone. Lips move only for the scripted lines. Every spoken line sits at the same close recording level from its first word to its last, and lines follow each other with no dead air.




ACCENT LOCK — each character has their own voice and they do not borrow from each other. RAPHAEL is the only person in this clip with a French accent: low, dry, precise, English with a clear French accent in all of his lines. LUCA speaks plain contemporary American English with no foreign colouring of any kind — a warm relaxed baritone, amused, unhurried; nothing French in his voice. LUCY speaks plain contemporary American English, soft and small; nothing French in her voice. REBECCA is silent.




DELIVERY: LUCA's "Luca is here." is slow and languid, stretched out with pleasure, landing on his own name, pitch falling at the end. His "Luca." is offered quietly, not announced. His "Come with me, Lucy." is warm and completely matter-of-fact, as if it had already been agreed. LUCY's "Lucy." is half the volume of everything else in the clip and slightly unsteady.




INTONATION: RAPHAEL's last line ends on a rising tag — "yes" lifts in pitch like a question tacked onto an instruction, warm and offhand, and he keeps walking through it.




SFX: boots on stone, the flip phone's snap, paper against wool, glossy covers sliding against each other as the stack changes hands, a chair sliding back, RAPHAEL's shoes crossing stone, a door opening and closing at the far end of the room. Under the three slow-motion shots there is only quiet room tone and the boots. LUCY's breathing is audible under the close shots and catches when he touches her hair.




POSITIVE LOCKS




Only four people exist in this clip. Segments 1 and 2 show LUCA only from behind; his face is first seen from the front in segment 4 as a strip across the eyes and whole in segment 5. LUCA's arms open on the move and stay open through his whole line, and both hands are empty when they do. RAPHAEL places both hands over LUCY's hands in segment 7 while she securely supports the magazines against her chest; he begins speaking before the gesture is complete. LUCY stays rooted on one spot on the open floor from the first frame until 25.2s and takes exactly one step, at the very end. LUCA never walks around her and never circles her. LUCY's face is clearly readable in every shot she appears in. REBECCA stands up and leaves during segment 6, seen only from behind, and does not appear again. RAPHAEL leaves through the far doorway at 17.5s and does not appear again. LUCY securely supports the magazines until 20.1s, transfers them to LUCA from 20.1s to 21.7s, and has empty hands from 21.7s to the end. There is exactly one table in the room and nothing stands against the walls. LUCY has no glasses on her face and none in her hands at any moment; nothing is slung or strapped to her forearms or shoulders. The stone floor is clean and clear everywhere. Every surface is blank and unbranded. Only the quoted lines are spoken. Clean picture with no on-screen text, subtitles, captions, titles or watermark. There is no music in any second of this clip.




LOOK: mid-2000s 35mm feature-film photography on fine-grain colour negative, warm-timed print: honey-caramel skin and wood, clean daylight at the windows, gently lifted blacks, glossy fashion-magazine saturation on dark fabric, chrome and lips. Light diffusion-filter glow: highlights and chrome bloom softly, skin reads smooth, gentle halation on bright edges. Rectilinear lenses, straight verticals. Shallow depth of field with creamy round bokeh wherever a face is the subject. Real 24 fps motion blur on the real-time shots. Fully photoreal live action, mid-2000s New York: the only telephones in this world are corded landlines and slim silver flip phones.
```
