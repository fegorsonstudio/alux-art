```
SCENE CONTEXT




The head stylist brings the accountant into the dressing room, guides her to the makeup chair and sits her facing the mirror. Standing behind her, he rests both hands on her shoulders, looks at her face in the mirror and tells her they are about to begin. Cut to black.




ACTIVE REFERENCES




@Image 3 LUCA: man, early 30s, head stylist, easy and warm, carrying the magazine stack from the previous scene until he sets it on the counter at 2.0s, then hands empty; standing throughout. Face, hair and outfit 100% match the reference.




@Image 2 LUCY: woman, 23, junior accountant, hair down and no glasses throughout; pale blue cardigan over a light tank, dark checked skirt, and the same shoes and necklace as the saved character sheet. No bag. Hands empty, resting in her lap once she sits; nervous, sitting as if at a job interview. Face, hair and outfit 100% match the reference.




LOCATION MAP




A small dressing room. Along one wall: a long white makeup counter with brushes, powders, hair tools and a wide mirror framed by round warm vanity bulbs; one black leather swivel makeup chair in front of it, 0.8 m from the mirror. Opposite wall: a chrome rack of black and white dresses. The door is at the far end of the room. A tall window at the side of the room lets in low late-afternoon sun. Segment 1: camera in the room's corner opposite the counter; the door screen-left, the chair and the counter screen-right. Segment 2: camera faces the pair from the mirror's side, looking straight back at them as the mirror sees them; the mirror itself is out of frame.




FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING




Frame one already holds both: LUCA x 35%, a light hand at LUCY's elbow, LUCY half a step behind him at x 25%, both just inside the door, walking screen-right toward the chair at x 70%.




Segment 2: LUCY seated center, x 50%, face at y 58%, square to the lens; LUCA standing directly behind her chair, x 50%, his face at y 25%, above and behind hers, leaning slightly forward; the dress rack a warm blur behind them.




FORMAT MODE




Controlled two-segment sequence with one HARD CUT at 3.5s, then a hard cut to black at 9.0s. Real-time motion. Dialogue clip.




OPTICS




Segment 1: 47° diagonal field of view, standard normal lens character, camera 3.5 m from the chair at 1.4 m height; natural proportions, the whole small room readable, the vanity bulbs glowing.




Segment 2: 47° standard normal, camera 1.8 m in front of LUCY at her seated eye level, straight on; both faces sharp, the room behind them falling into creamy warm bokeh.




CAMERA




Segment 1: locked-off tripod, horizon level. Segment 2: slow dolly push-in of 20 cm across the whole segment, horizon locked, the pair centered.




ACTION TIMING




0.0s to 1.2s — LUCA leads LUCY from the door to the chair, a light hand at her elbow; she follows half a step behind, looking around the room.




1.2s to 2.0s — at the chair he sets the magazines he took from her in the previous scene onto the counter, leaving both his hands free.




2.0s to 3.0s — he draws the chair back; she sits on its front edge, knees together, hands in her lap.




3.0s to 3.5s — he turns the chair a quarter turn to face the mirror.




3.5s HARD CUT




3.5s to 4.3s — LUCY looks at herself in the mirror, straight into the lens, shoulders up near her ears; LUCA stands behind her chair.




4.3s to 5.0s — his hands come down onto her shoulders, both hands, light and steady; her shoulders drop a centimeter as she lets out a breath.




5.2s to 7.0s — LUCA looks into the lens, at her face in the mirror, a small warm smile: "Well then. Let's begin, muse."




7.0s to 9.0s — LUCY's eyes lift from her own reflection to his, just above the lens; a small breath; the corner of her mouth moves, almost a smile. His hands stay on her shoulders.




9.0s HARD CUT TO BLACK




9.0s to 10.0s — pure black frame.




ACTING TASK — LUCA (fully invested; the work is in his eyes):




SCENE DIRECTION (shared, unspoken): keep the machine running.




MOTIVE: he has seen his boss find muses before; this one is simply frightened.




GOAL: make her feel she is in safe hands before anyone touches her hair.




OBSTACLE: she sits like a person waiting for a verdict.




TACTIC: he settles her into the chair, then gives her something steady: the hands on her shoulders are an anchor. He borrows his boss's word "muse" with a small smile, so it becomes a joke they share rather than a title she has to live up to.




ACTING TASK — LUCY:




MOTIVE: she still does not know what she agreed to.




GOAL: be good at this, because being useful is the only thing she knows how to do here.




OBSTACLE: the girl in the mirror is still the accountant.




TACTIC: she checks her reflection the way she checks a figure; when his hands land she lets herself be held; on "muse" she searches his eyes in the mirror for the joke, finds only kindness, and almost smiles.




(Safety: gazes engaged, natural blink cadence, listeners' mouths closed.)




PHYSICS




The swivel chair turns with her weight in it and settles. The magazine stack has weight and settles on the counter. His hands settle with weight on the cardigan; the knit compresses under his fingers. Her shoulders drop as the breath leaves her; the cardigan shifts with them. Her loose hair rests naturally around her face and over her shoulders.




LIGHTING




Primary source: the round warm vanity bulbs around the mirror, lighting both faces softly from the front; round bulb catchlights in both pairs of eyes; her eyes clearly visible with no glasses. Secondary: low late-afternoon sun from the side window, a warm rim on her hair and on his shoulder. The room behind them in soft warm shadow. Exposed for skin.




AUDIO




Small-room tone, the faint hum of the vanity bulbs, the muffled bustle of the studio beyond the door. SFX: the chair casters, the magazine stack on the counter, the chair turning. LUCA: warm relaxed baritone, soft, almost private. LUCY is silent. Lips move only for the scripted line. At 9.0s all sound cuts with the picture into silence. Score is added in the edit.




POSITIVE LOCKS




Only LUCA and LUCY are in the room. LUCY has her hair down, no glasses and no bag throughout; her hands are empty and rest in her lap once she sits. LUCA stands behind her chair with both hands on her shoulders from 4.3s to 9.0s. In segment 2 both faces are frontal to the lens and the mirror itself stays out of frame. Only the quoted line is spoken. The clip ends on a pure black frame from 9.0s to 10.0s. Clean picture without on-screen text.




LOOK: mid-2000s 35mm feature-film photography. Fine Kodak Vision2 negative grain, warm-timed print: honey-caramel skin and wood, clean slightly cool daylight at the windows, gently lifted blacks, glossy fashion-magazine saturation on black fabric, chrome and lips. Light diffusion-filter glow: window highlights, vanity bulbs and chrome bloom softly, skin reads smooth, gentle halation on bright edges. Rectilinear lenses, straight verticals. Shallow depth of field with creamy round bokeh wherever a face is the subject. Real 24 fps motion blur. Fully photoreal live action, mid-2000s New York: the only phones in this world are corded landlines and slim silver flip phones.
```
