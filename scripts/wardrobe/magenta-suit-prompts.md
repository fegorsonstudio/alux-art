# The Magenta Boardroom — 10 prompts for Google Flow

Template `06cae1e1-9a2c-4a77-957d-b0101c92307c` (draft). Paste one prompt per
generation. The outfit, backdrop and person stay identical across all ten; only
pose, framing, camera angle and expression change.

## Before you paste anything

**Attach exactly three references, opening the picker once per reference.** The
picker closes after each selection, so choosing three in one open silently
attaches only the last one.

| Slot | File |
|---|---|
| Identity | `identity1.jpg`, `identity2.jpg.jpg`, `identity3.jpg` (all three) |
| Outfit | the magenta waistcoat / black trousers ghost-mannequin sheet |
| Backdrop | the water-caustics dark studio image |

**Do not attach a pose photograph.** A pose reference puts that person's face
into the output; every prompt below describes the pose in words instead.

Shoes, nails and jewellery are described in the text rather than attached, so
the three attachment slots stay free for the things that must be exact.

---

## The block that goes on every prompt

Paste this at the end of each of the ten prompts. It is the same every time.

```
Constraints:
PRESERVE — the exact person from the identity references: face shape, eye
spacing, nose shape and nose ring, lips, jawline, cheekbones, skin tone and
texture, close-cropped hair, ear piercings, and body build. She must be
recognisably the same individual in every frame.
PRESERVE — the outfit from the outfit reference, element for element: the deep
magenta sleeveless waistcoat with its curved neckline, four round gold buttons
in a single front row, princess seams, two welt pockets at the waist, and the
short flared peplum; the black wide-leg trousers with a pressed centre crease
falling to the floor. Do not restyle, recolour, add sleeves, change the button
count, or substitute a different garment.
PRESERVE — the backdrop from the backdrop reference: a dark studio corner with
rippling water-light caustics across the walls and a shallow reflecting pool
underfoot.
CHANGE — pose, camera angle, framing, expression and composition only.
AVOID — the white vest, pink shorts or grey wall from the identity photographs;
those are capture context, not wardrobe. No extra jewellery beyond what is
described. No text, watermark, logo or graphic overlay. No extra fingers or
malformed hands. No beauty smoothing that removes skin texture.
```

## 1 — Full length, standing, front

```

subject. Single hard key from high camera-left, deep falloff to near-black at
the frame edges.
Subject: A tall slim woman with deep brown skin and close-cropped hair,
standing square to camera at full length, weight on her back foot, chin level,
arms relaxed at her sides, a composed and unsmiling expression.

pointed leather pumps. A gold Rolex Datejust with a fluted bezel, champagne dial
```
